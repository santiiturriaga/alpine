    FIELD GUIDE

    Ski it like a local.

    01 / The controls

    ADEdge & carve — press into the turn
    WPush on flats, tuck at speed.
    BSwitch between skis and boots. Use W and S to walk.
    SBrake. At speed, turn both skis across the slope. Use A or D to choose the side.
    ClickSwing a pole while standing, walking, or gliding slowly.
    SPACEOllie. Hit the lips, catch the air.
    SHIFTHold in the air to grab.
    ADWSPress after takeoff to spin or flip.
    RReturn to the Roundhouse unload.
    EBoard a lift, enter a vehicle or observe nearby wildlife. Press again to skip a lift or leave a parked vehicle.
    CVC changes the camera. V switches between a gondola cabin and its exterior view. Click the snow to look freely with the mouse.
    LVisit the outfitter for skis, clothing and field notes.
    MOpen the trail atlas. Scroll or pinch to zoom and drag to pan.
    ESCPause to reopen this guide or turn on the nearby trail map.
    GChoose a destination, season, storm or challenge.

    02 / The mountain

    Follow the bright corduroy for speed and coins. Drop into the trees to find five hidden glades — deep stashes marked only by small wooden signs and a faint glow.

    Blue and black runs hold natural airs and kickers. Land with your skis pointed down the fall line. Keep the rotation clean. The mountain is forgiving; style is not.

    PRO TIPCoins build a streak. A streak multiplies every point you earn — chain them through a whole run without stopping.

    03 / Etiquette

    Ride within your ability. Give downhill skiers room. Look before you drop. If you find the stash, leave some for the next person.
