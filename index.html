      Field Guide

      Touch controls
      Left and right arrows
      Hold to steer.
      Up and down arrows
      Hold up to move forward or tuck, and down to brake or walk backward.
      Jump button
      Tap to jump. Hold a steering arrow in the air to spin.
      Walk or Ski
      Switch between boots and skis.
      Lift prompt
      Tap the lift name to board. Tap Skip to reach the other station.
      Camera
      Tap to change view. Drag on the snow to look around.
      Trail map
      Drag to pan and pinch to zoom.
      Mountain
      Open travel, conditions and activities.
      Pause
      Resume skiing or change audio, graphics and view settings.
      Keyboard & mouse

      Skiing

      AD
      Carve left or right.
      W
      Push off on flats. Hold to tuck once you are moving.
      S
      Brake. At speed, add A or D to pick the side of your hockey stop.
      B
      Switch between skis and boots. Walk with W and S.
      Click
      Swing a pole while standing, walking or gliding slowly.

      Jumps & tricks

      Space
      Jump from a lip or off the snow.
      Shift
      Hold in the air to grab.
      ADWS
      Press after takeoff to spin or flip.

      Getting around

      E
      Interact with a nearby lift, vehicle or animal. Press again to skip the ride or leave a parked vehicle.
      Q
      Select another lift when several are close.
      C
      Change the camera. Click or drag on the snow to look around.
      V
      Switch between a gondola cabin and the exterior view.
      R
      Return to Roundhouse Lodge.
      L
      Open the outfitter.
      M
      Open the trail map. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom.
      G
      Open the mountain menu to travel, change conditions or start a challenge.
      Esc
      Pause and open settings.

      On the mountain

      Follow groomed runs for coins. Look for signs and a faint glow near the five hidden glades.

      Land with your skis pointed downhill. Give downhill skiers room and look before you drop in.

      Collect coins in a row to build a score multiplier.
