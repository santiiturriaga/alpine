# International resort landmarks

These structures use hand-built geometry in the same local metre grids as the resort trails and lifts. Find them under **Resort → Quick travel** or in the trail map’s landmark picker. Source photographs are references for the models.

The two summit terrain patches use metre-scale triplanar granite shading on exposed slopes. Stone joints, concrete weathering and the variation between panes are procedural finishes based on the reference photographs.

## Galzigbahn valley station, Ski Arlberg

The glass and triangulated steel shell follows the long axis of the cable line in St. Anton. OpenStreetMap way 193437922 sets the plan envelope, approximately 67 m long and 24 m wide on the main apron. The glazed shell rises toward the uphill cable opening, above sloping concrete sidewalls. The two visible 9 m raise wheels are based on the manufacturer’s installation description. The lift carries riders along the game’s existing loading and cable path. The exposed wheels are fixed scenery. Glazing panel sizes, concrete grades, the varying section heights and the wall openings are photo-based estimates.

[driendl architects’ photo gallery and site plan](https://driendl.at/en/projects/galzigbahn) · [Doppelmayr installation footage and Funitel dimensions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R3BDOksP4FA) · [OpenStreetMap station footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/193437922)

## Mont Fort panorama platform, Verbier / 4 Vallées

The stairs climb from the cable-car terminal toward the glass-edged terrace on the southeast side of the summit. Seven flights, 130 treads, steel grating, glass panels and the double-barred summit cross follow Morand’s construction photographs and specification. The stair centreline and asymmetric terrace outline were traced from SWISSIMAGE and reprojected into UTM zone 32N. The terminal follows OpenStreetMap way 208449395. Deck height, individual tread rise, steel members and substructure are estimates. The game uses an interpolated walking surface over the stairs.

[mjd architectes site photos](https://www.mjd.ch/plateforme-panoramique-mont-fort/) · [Morand construction record and photos](https://morand-sa.ch/projet/mont-fort-experience-nendaz/) · [SWISSIMAGE orthophotos](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/orthoimage-swissimage-10) · [OpenStreetMap station footprint](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/208449395)

The 2 m, 121 × 121 terrain patch is a bilinear resample of the 2024 swissALTI3D tile 2590-1103, from LV95 into the resort grid. Its bounds are x 860–1100 m and z 2860–3100 m, with a 46 m blend into the broad resort terrain. Source: Federal Office of Topography swisstopo.

## Aiguille du Midi, Chamonix–Mont-Blanc

The model includes the north cable-car station and its terraces, a 17.6 m footbridge between the rock pinnacles, the upper panorama decks, the 29 m TDF spire and the glazed Pas dans le Vide box. The lift approach follows the mapped cable axis. The station’s cable height uses the operator’s 3,777 m arrival level plus clearance. The steel span endpoints follow OpenStreetMap way 231707009. The mast and glass box follow mapped points. North station massing, glass panes, terrace elevations and rail placement are simplified from the imagery and mapped outlines. The north lift arrival, connecting bridge and upper terrace have walking surfaces. Use Quick travel to reach the 3,842 m terrace.

[Operator’s summit plan](https://www.compagniedumontblanc.fr/Marketing/plans/hiver/Plan_Hiver_TAM.pdf) · [Chamonix tourism office photos](https://www.chamonix.com/sites/default/files/media/brochures/GuideOrganisateurFR2024.pdf) · [Operator’s bridge replacement footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fcaah9rZDpk) · [OpenStreetMap North station](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/231374774) · [Footbridge](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/231707009) · [TDF tower](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/13295769536)

The 1 m, 221 × 221 local terrain patch comes from IGN’s LiDAR HD bare-earth GeoTIFF WMS layer `IGNF_LIDAR-HD_MNT_ELEVATION.ELEVATIONGRIDCOVERAGE.LAMB93`. The bounds are x 1480–1700 m and z 3280–3500 m, with a 40 m blend into the broad resort terrain. The rock under the North station has been interpolated where building classification obscures it. Source: IGN, Licence Ouverte 2.0.
